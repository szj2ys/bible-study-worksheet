Bible study worksheet

A place for careful reading, honest questions, and a practical response.

1. Read in context

Who is speaking, to whom, and in what situation? Check the surrounding chapter.

2. Notice what the text says

List repeated words, contrasts, commands, images, or changes. Keep observations separate from your interpretation.

3. Ask and check

What is unclear? Note another passage, a trusted reference, or a question for your study leader. Record the source of any explanation.

4. Respond thoughtfully

What might you do, reconsider, discuss, or pray about? A reflection is your response to the passage, not a new verse.

Personal study notes. Check quotations and interpretations against the passage and its context.

How to use this worksheet
  1. Choose a short passage and write down the translation you use.
  2. Read the surrounding context before filling in the four sections.
  3. Use “Save worksheet file” to keep an editable JSON copy. “Open saved file” restores it on this device or another one.
  4. Use your browser's print dialog to print or save as a PDF. Leave the fields empty to print a blank worksheet.

This tool does not interpret Scripture, generate answers, or store your work on a server. Your writing may include private information: keep downloaded files somewhere appropriate and avoid shared-device downloads. Closing or refreshing the tab discards unsaved changes.

Made by TrustBible, a Bible reading and question resource. This worksheet works independently and sends it no notes. Use your chosen Bible edition and trusted study resources; the worksheet does not replace pastoral guidance.
